# CAD: parametric Green Lantern power battery (rev C)

The enclosure is generated by CadQuery from `params.py`. Board placement and component dimensions are shared with the board drawing through `docs/review/protoboard/layout.py` and `mechanical.py`. Regenerate the board drawing after changing those files, then rebuild the CAD.

```bash
pip install cadquery trimesh manifold3d numpy pillow networkx scipy shapely
python3 build.py            # all parts -> output/*.stl + *.step, output/assembly.step, fit checks
python3 build.py sphere     # one module (base, bells, electronics, foot, handle, socket, sphere, top)
python3 tests/test_socket.py      # rotor sweep: collisions, hard stops, lever contact
python3 tests/test_electronics.py # battery, strap, board clearance in the cap and collision negative controls
python3 tests/test_inserts.py     # every insert bore and thumbscrew insert in the real solids, every screw shank, hidden heads
python3 render_views.py     # docs/images/cad-*.png (iso, front, rear, top, cutaway, cartridge, cap, exploded, parts sheet)
python3 render_stl.py output ../docs/images/stl   # per-part previews + parts-iso.png contact sheet
python3 export_viewer.py    # docs/assembly-viewer/index.html, the 3D assembly viewer (meshes inlined)
python3 make_videos.py      # docs/videos/*.mp4 from the viewer
python3 thumb_test.py       # output/thumb_test.stl: 6-32 insert hole test (4.6 / 4.8 / 5.0 mm)
python3 emblem_jig.py       # output/emblem_jig.stl: centres the emblem's middle disc while it is glued
python3 curtain_stencil.py  # output/curtain_stencil.stl: cutting stencil for the one-piece hand-side cover
python3 ring_sizer.py       # output/ring_sizer.stl: ten test bands to choose RING_ID for key_ring
python3 render_ring_colour.py  # docs/images/ring-white-outline.png: key_ring with its white emblem outline
```

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `params.py` | every dimension and the stack heights (`Z_*`); fastener sizes (M3, the 6-32 thumbscrews and their inserts, the green screw caps) |
| `DESIGN_CONTRACT.md` | how the parts mate; revisions 2-9 and C record the changes since the first draft |
| `parts/sphere.py` | the 224 mm sphere halves (lens opening in front, 140 mm hand opening behind, collars for the bells, thumbscrew inserts at both flats, pivot pads), bay floor, guide boss, glass well, nozzle guide, lock wall, the white `led_panel`, glass / glass-path / fist / hand-path envelopes |
| `parts/bells.py` | front bell (LED ring, frosted `diffuser` lens), rear bell (LED ring, one-piece frosted cover held by the full `curtain_clamp` ring); thumbscrew inserts in their throats |
| `parts/top.py` | `top_cap` (plug, neck, flare, cap, lid grooves), `cap_floor` (battery, board, buck, S1, charge socket), `halo_ring`, `emblem_lid`, `emblem_centre`, `wall_extension` |
| `parts/foot.py` | the flared foot between the drum and the sphere; its riser holds the bay floor |
| `parts/base.py` | the 80 mm drum (`base`, part of the shell), the one-piece cartridge (`base_tray`: pump saddle, splash wall, draining bag bay, tube clip), `bag_cover`, `screw_cap`, pump / bag envelopes |
| `parts/handle.py` | loop handle in four pieces on two ring pivots (printed axles by default), lap joints; `NOTES-handle.md` |
| `parts/socket.py` | the lock: housing, rotor, base plate with two mounting ears, switch bracket, plunger, key ring (and key cap for a bought ring); screwed to the lock wall facing the hand opening |
| `parts/electronics.py` | non-printing board, component, connector and wire-space envelopes on the cap floor; coordinates follow the board assembly drawing |
| `checks.py` | validity, hardware and hand envelope clearance, the glass's way out, part overlap (> 0.1 cm3 fails), handle swing, electronics |
| `output/` | ready-to-slice STL (and STEP) files |

Assembly order and the print table are in `docs/BUILD_GUIDE.md`.

## Hardware envelopes to verify against the real parts
- Kamoer KPHM200: measured by Kevin on 2026-10-23: head 2.5 x 2.5 in (63.5 mm square), silver motor can 40 mm
  across and ~50 mm long, 114 mm overall from the motor tabs. Modelled as a 64 x 64 x 40 mm head box plus a 40 mm
  can (`PUMP_*` in `params.py`), on the cartridge at `PUMP_X` = 37 mm, its head in a U-saddle and one strap round the
  motor. The head box's length (`PUMP_HEAD_L_EST` in `parts/base.py`) is a 40 mm allowance.
- Drink bag: a 32 oz spouted pouch (amazon.com/dp/B09WR13XDT) lying in the D-shaped bay beside the pump under
  `bag_cover` (`bag_envelope`, about 0.65 L of room, so it fills to ~22 oz). The floor falls 2 deg to a well in the back
  corner (`WELL_XY`); the tube's notched tip, still inside the bag, presses into the C-clip there (`CLIP_*`).
- Battery: [TalentCell YB1203000](https://talentcell.com/upload/download/12v-user-manual.pdf), 105 x 65 x 25 mm
  (`POWERBANK_*`), on the cap floor (`PACK_CAP_XY`), LED face down over a slot so its fuel gauge shows looking up
  the neck; one 20 mm hook-and-loop strap in a <= 2 mm recess (`BATTERY_STRAP_T`).
- Shot glass: 40 mm base, 52 mm top, 62 mm tall envelope in the 54 mm wide `glass_well` slot.
- Hand: a 106 x 76 mm fist (owner's is under 4 x 3 in), 85 mm long, ring face on the lock, through the 140 mm
  hand opening and the rear bell.
- Kevin's knurled thumbscrews (6-32 PC-case thread, 6.5 mm thread, 7 x 2.6 mm collar, 11 x 8.7 mm head) and the
  initeq #6-32 long heat-set inserts (`THUMB_*`, `THUMB_INSERT_*`): print `thumb_test.py` to pick the insert hole.
- Hall sensor (`HALL_SENSOR`): the A3144 lies face up in a 4.4 x 3.4 mm pocket on a post on `socket_base`, inside
  a blind hole in the rotor, ~3 mm from the magnet.

## Fasteners (nothing shows from outside)
- `M3_INSERT_HOLE = 4.0`, `M3_INSERT_DEPTH = 6.5`: 17 M3 heat-set inserts. `THUMB_INSERT_HOLE = 4.8`,
  `THUMB_INSERT_DEPTH = 7.0`: 13 6-32 inserts, one under every thumbscrew. `tests/test_inserts.py` checks every bore
  in the actual solids (depth, surrounding plastic, closed end), every screw shank through its parts, that every
  thumbscrew head and collar clears everything, and that the bell thumbscrews' heads hide behind the opening's edge.
- Thumbscrews turn from inside: the bells' (parallel to the hand, heads inside the sphere), the top cap's (inside
  its neck), the foot's (inside its neck) and the cap floor's (under the lid).
- M3 heads that would show at an angle sink under green `screw_cap` discs (drum 3, handle laps 18, pivots 4).
- `LED_RING = False` in `parts/socket.py`: no LEDs at the socket.

## Known limitations
- The sphere halves print dome up; the pads behind the bell thumbscrews have a short flat overhang under the
  collar's seat (a little droop does not matter).
- `key_ring` is sized by `RING_ID` (finger hole, 20.5 mm). Its raised emblem is the key: the rotor pocket is the
  emblem's negative (no tab). The magnet sits behind the centre disc under `RING_MAGNET_SKIN` (0.6 mm). The fit
  checks skip it against the fist and the glass path (the hand is withdrawn before the glass comes out) and against
  `key_cap` (the alternative for the same pocket).

## Electronics fit limits

![The cap floor](../docs/images/cad-cap.png)

Everything electrical except the pump and the LED strips sits on `cap_floor`, inside the top cap under the lid.
`build.py` checks the envelopes against printed parts and other hardware, with a 0.5 mm³ overlap limit for
electronics. Boolean errors and failed checks return a nonzero exit status. Individual component bodies also require
at least 1 mm separation and clear the Ø6 mm screw-head/access areas. The saved 66 rear-wire/cable corridors are
checked at 1.6 mm insulated diameter. See the [board guide](../docs/review/protoboard/README.md#dimensions-used) for
dimensional sources and limits. These are clearance allowances, not detailed supplier CAD or measured parts:

| Item | Model / assembly limit |
|---|---|
| Perfboard | 70 x 50 x 1.2 mm, component face up on four 10 mm standoffs (`BOARD_STANDOFF`) with M3 inserts; rear wires fit in the 10 mm under it, outside the four screw areas. |
| XIAO and U1 | 12 mm projection including XIAO headers; 10.5 mm for U1 and socket. The USB cable reaches it with the lid off. |
| Buck | 43 x 21 x 14 mm above four 2 mm printed corner supports, two cable ties; 10 mm lead allowance beyond each end. Check the delivered module. |
| Lock/LED/pump inline plugs | 22 x 20 x 16 mm over the cap floor's wire hole (`CAP_WIRE_HOLE_XY`): unplug them to lift the cap floor right out. |
| S1 | Hangs from the roof of a printed box on the plate, lever up through a 6.5 mm hole; body <= 13 x 9 x 18 mm. Verify the actual switch. |
| Charge socket | The splitter's female end, tied along X on the plate (`CHARGE_SOCKET_XY`); plug the charger in with the lid off. |
| Battery plug | The splitter's straight male plug, Ø12 mm, 28.6 mm out of the port, its cord in a U-turn and up (`battery_connector_envelope`). The pack's rocker stays ON. |
| D2 / C4 | D2: across the motor terminals beyond the motor's end, on the cartridge. C4: behind the fixed lock wall's Hall window. |
| D2F-01L | Existing D2F-family body/lever model and bracket retained. Check the supplied switch's terminals, lever, peg spacing and action. |

Cable envelopes cover local exits and connector bends, not the full harness. Leave enough slack in the leads up
the neck to lift the cap floor, or unplug the inline plugs first.
