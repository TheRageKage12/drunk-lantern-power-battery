# CAD: parametric Green Lantern power battery

The enclosure is generated by CadQuery from `params.py`. Board placement and component dimensions are shared with the board drawing through `docs/review/protoboard/layout.py` and `mechanical.py`. Regenerate the board drawing after changing those files, then rebuild the CAD.

```bash
pip install cadquery trimesh manifold3d numpy pillow networkx scipy shapely
python3 build.py            # all parts -> output/*.stl + *.step, output/assembly.step, fit checks
python3 build.py socket     # one module (base, dishes, sphere, socket, handle)
python3 tests/test_socket.py   # rotor sweep: collisions, hard stops, lever contact
python3 tests/test_electronics.py  # battery, strap, board clearance and collision negative controls
python3 render_views.py     # docs/images/cad-*.png (iso, front, rear, top, cutaway, exploded, parts sheet)
python3 render_stl.py output ../docs/images/stl   # per-part previews + parts-iso.png contact sheet
python3 export_viewer.py    # docs/assembly-viewer/index.html, the 3D assembly viewer (meshes inlined)
```

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `params.py` | every dimension and the stack heights (`Z_*`) |
| `DESIGN_CONTRACT.md` | how the parts mate; revisions 2-9 record the changes since the first draft (8 = lock wall layout, 9 = base cartridge, battery, Hall post, nozzle, 190 mm base) |
| `parts/base.py` | base drum (part of the lantern shell) and the removable cartridge: tray, deck (jar ring, pump saddles, electronics panel), thumbscrew knobs, hardware envelopes |
| `parts/electronics.py` | non-printing board, component, connector and wire-space envelopes; coordinates follow the board assembly drawing |
| `parts/dishes.py` | lower and upper reflector dishes with the spigot / locating rings; `top_diffuser` (frosted disc) in the upper dish floor; pilots for the curtain clamp; `NOTES-dishes.md` |
| `parts/sphere.py` | sphere halves (hand opening, display window), diffuser, bay floor, glass slot, nozzle guide, lock wall, curtain clamp, handle bosses, glass / glass-path / fist / forearm envelopes |
| `parts/socket.py` | the lock: housing, rotor, base plate with two mounting ears, switch bracket, plunger, key cap; screwed to the lock wall facing the hand opening (`LED_RING = True` adds a NeoPixel ring recess and halo) |
| `parts/handle.py` | four-piece handle with lap joints; `NOTES-handle.md` |
| `checks.py` | validity, hardware and hand envelope clearance, the glass's way out, part overlap (> 0.1 cm3 fails) |
| `output/` | ready-to-slice STL (and STEP) files |

Assembly order and the print table are in `docs/BUILD_GUIDE.md` section 3 and 9.

## Hardware envelopes to verify against the real parts
- Kamoer KPHM200: modelled as a 64 x 63 x 40 mm head box plus a 40 mm motor can, 118 mm overall. Kamoer
  publishes no drawing. Measure the pump and adjust `PUMP_*` in `params.py` and `PUMP_HEAD_L_EST` in `parts/base.py`.
- Reservoir jar: a 76 x 76 mm envelope (`BOTTLE_D`, `BOTTLE_H`); buy one at most 73 mm across the cap (`BOTTLE_NECK_D`) and 76 mm tall. The ring on the deck is `BOTTLE_D` + 1.
- Battery: [TalentCell YB1203000](https://talentcell.com/upload/download/12v-user-manual.pdf), 105 × 65 × 25 mm (`POWERBANK_*`), connector/switch end toward -Y, away from the hidden S1 pocket. The 20 mm strap is required; the deck remains at z=44 mm independently of battery thickness, with 17 mm above the case. Strap slots at y=25 clear the pillars; the underside recess accepts a <=1 mm strap. Nothing shows on the shell: S1 stays underneath, charge jack on the deck.
- Shot glass: 40 mm base, 52 mm top, 62 mm tall envelope in the 54 mm wide `glass_well` slot.
- Hand: a 106 x 76 mm fist (owner's is under 4 x 3 in), 85 mm long, ring face on the lock, plus a 60 mm forearm through the 120 x 96 mm hand opening.
- Hall sensor (`HALL_SENSOR`): the A3144 lies face up in a 4.4 x 3.4 mm pocket on a post on `socket_base`, inside a blind hole in the rotor, ~3 mm from the magnet. With `'DRV5032'` the board goes in a 31.6 x 18.4 mm pocket under the plate instead (~8 mm from the magnet).

## Budget-build settings
- `M3_INSERT_HOLE = 4.0`, `M3_INSERT_DEPTH = 6.5` in `params.py`: 32 receiving holes for M3-thread brass heat-set inserts. Screw clearance holes and captured-nut joints keep their existing dimensions. Install inserts before assembly; see the part-by-part count in the build guide.
- `tests/test_inserts.py` checks every receiving bore in the actual solids, including its depth, surrounding plastic and closed end. The nozzle-guide boss has a matching relief in the lower dish; the bay floor prints upside down with support under its recessed floor.
- `LED_RING = False` in `parts/socket.py`: no LEDs at the socket.

## Known limitations
- The sphere halves need interior supports around the window and the hand opening, and the two dishes need a short
  ring of build-plate support under their bearing faces (see the guide's part table).
- The rear sphere half is 221 mm wide because of the integral handle bosses.
- `key_ring.stl` (the kit ring with the key cap merged in) is not generated; glue `key_cap` to any ring.

## Electronics fit limits

![Updated cartridge](../docs/images/cad-cartridge.png)

The updated tray/deck retain the 190 mm outer base and all pump/jar/board heights. The charge jack moves to (74, -25) on the deck to clear the populated board with its charger plugged in. Four deck mounting
pads now stay inside the board's 9 × 9 mm screw areas; print supports beneath them and remove these
before installing the board. Their former sloped undersides intruded into rear wiring space.

`build.py` checks the envelopes against printed parts, other hardware and the continuous shell-boss
removal sweep, with a 0.5 mm³ overlap limit for electronics. Boolean errors and failed checks return a
nonzero exit status. Individual component bodies also require at least 1 mm separation and clear the Ø6 mm screw-head/access areas. The saved 66 rear-wire/cable corridors are checked at 1.6 mm insulated diameter with explicit crossing layers and terminal rises. See the [board guide](../docs/review/protoboard/README.md#dimensions-used) for dimensional sources and limits. These are clearance allowances, not detailed supplier CAD or measured parts:

| Item | Model / assembly limit |
|---|---|
| Perfboard | 70 × 50 × 1.2 mm, landscape; bottom 2 mm above deck, components outward. Mount holes follow the board drawing. Panel moves inward 5.5 mm while the board stays put; rear wires fit within 10 mm, outside the four screw areas. |
| XIAO and U1 | 12 mm projection including XIAO headers; 10.5 mm for U1 and socket. USB cable is attached with the shell off; detach the board if the plug needs more room. |
| C2 | Case <=25 mm long × Ø18 mm, laid flat and secured as drawn. [Vetco's listing](https://vetco.net/products/2200uf25vdc_2200uf_25_volt_electrolytic_capacitor) does not specify the case dimensions. |
| Q1/Q2 and small parts | MOSFET projection <=22 mm; sensor resistors <=3.2 mm, diode <=3.4 mm. Keep edge parts within the drawing's individual bounds so the retaining boss can pass. |
| Battery plug / switch face | Full case width, 25 mm beyond the -Y end, z=7–31 mm reserved for connector, wire bend and switch operation. Verify the supplied lead fits; this is not a measured plug model. Deck can be unscrewed for service. |
| Buck | 43 × 21 × 14 mm above four 2 mm printed corner supports; 10 mm lead allowance beyond each end. Check the delivered module and ties. |
| Lock/LED inline plugs | Combined 20 × 64 × 16 mm space above the unused right-side module pocket (x=47–67, y=-32–32, z=10–26); secure leads clear of the sliding shell bosses. |
| S1 / charge jack | S1 body + terminals <=13 × 9 × 18 mm above pocket ceiling, nut Ø11 mm, stem/lever within Ø6.2 mm to z=7. Jack body/terminals <=12 × 12 × 20 mm below deck, Ø7.8 mm bushing, Ø12 × 3 mm nut above. Charging plug allowance: Ø12 × 30 mm above the nut, then 10 mm for its cable exit. Verify actual parts. |
| D2 / C4 | D2: 20 × 10 × 8 mm including insulation beyond motor end. C4: 8 × 6 × 8 mm including joints behind the fixed lock wall's Hall window; keep clear of rotor. |
| D2F-01L | Existing D2F-family body/lever model and bracket retained. Check the supplied switch's terminals, lever, peg spacing and action; existing rotor test covers the model, not supplier authenticity. |

Cable envelopes cover local exits and connector bends, not the full off-board harness. Board screw access is checked with the shell removed; other service/tool access still needs a dry fit. Wire paths do not specify or verify a manufacturer bend radius.
Tie the remaining leads to the cartridge and manually check the complete shell-removal motion before
powering. Pump dimensions remain estimated; fit checks cannot qualify unknown real-part dimensions.
