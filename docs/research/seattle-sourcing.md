## Decisions / recommendations

- **Electronics: go to Vetco Electronics, Bellevue.** It is the only confirmed-open walk-in component store in the metro. Confirmed in stock online (same inventory as the store): 1/4 W resistors, heat shrink, JST PH/SM/RCY pre-wired pairs ($2-3), 2.0 mm KHS/JST housings + crimp pins, perfboard ($3.49+), inline 3AG/5x20 fuse holders ($2.39-2.99), 5x20 mm fuses (5-pk $6.50), A3144EUA Hall sensor ($1.99), 74AHCT125 level shifter ($2.95), microswitches ($1.59 no-lever to $7.95 Honeywell lever; the two lever types in the Products table are $2.74-2.99), mini/sub-mini toggle switches ($1.99-4.99), 1 m WS2812 strip ($19.95). **Not stocked: Seeed XIAO (order from Amazon/Adafruit), neodymium disc magnets, heat-set inserts.**
- **Supertronix/Alphatronics (Tukwila), Radar Inc (Bothell): do not plan on them.** supertronix.com is a parked GoDaddy for-sale page; alphatronicsusa.com does not resolve; no post-2016 evidence either exists. Treat as closed.
- **Hardwick's (U-District) is gone.** Seattle store closed September 2020; the company is in Post Falls, Idaho, and its own site says the Post Falls brick-and-mortar store is "Opening 2027" (online only today).
- **Metric M3 fasteners in small counts: Tacoma Screw (Ballard or Georgetown branch, weekday-only hours, Ballard also Sat 9-1) is the best bet** for exact-spec M3 socket-head screws, nuts, washers by the piece or small bag. Backup: Stoneway Hardware Ballard ("fasteners by the piece or by the box"), McLendon (Renton / White Center), any Seattle Ace (Magnolia, Maple Leaf, Junction confirmed via Checkbook; Tweedy & Popp (unverified)). Vetco also sells M3 brass standoffs/screws individually ($0.25-0.99).
- **Heat-set inserts: no Seattle retail source found** (Vetco search returns none; hardware stores do not carry them - estimate). Buy online (Amazon pack, see Products).
- **Springs: Vetco sells Hillman compression springs individually ($1.29 ea, confirmed).** Ace/McLendon/Stoneway have Hillman/Century Spring drawers (standard for these chains, but not fetch-confirmed). No Seattle "spring shop" for one-off hobby springs was confirmed; McMaster-Carr online is the exact-spec fallback.
- **Magnets: K&J Magnetics online (D41 = 1/4 x 1/16 in, $0.28 ea; D42 = 1/4 x 1/8 in, $0.41 ea) or the Amazon SMARTAKE 6x2 mm 45-pack already on the shortlist.** Vetco has no neodymium discs. Home Depot/Lowe's Master Magnetics blister packs exist chain-wide (unconfirmed here, estimate).
- **Food-grade tubing / bottles: Micro Homebrew (Kenmore) is the only homebrew shop confirmed open.** Sound Homebrew Supply (Georgetown) - its domain now redirects to an unrelated hydraulics business; treat as closed until phoned. Mountain Homebrew (Kirkland) - its domain is now a generic blog; unconfirmed. Micro Homebrew's site does not list tubing sizes; call 425-892-8425 before driving. For the pump head itself use the correct 1/8 in ID x 1/4 in OD platinum silicone from Amazon; homebrew shops typically stock 3/16 in and 1/4 in ID silicone/vinyl by the foot (estimate).
- **Shot glasses / small squeeze bottles: Bargreen Ellingson Seattle showroom (SoDo, site says it serves "home kitchens and commercial kitchen professionals", Mon-Fri 10-3 only)**, Uwajimaya (daily 8-9), Container Store Bellevue, Daiso Westwood Village (West Seattle; address/hours unverified).
- **Big prints that exceed a 220 x 220 bed: KCLS Bellevue Library Makerspace is free** (no library card needed) with Prusa Core One (250 x 220 x 270 mm), MK4, MK3S and PETG on hand, 2 h/week reservation. Seattle Makers (Interbay) has Prusa MK4s/MK3S+ (members $75/mo plus paid machine time and a certification class, or $20 guest pass with a member). Online: JLC3DP (PLA "from $1.00", 72 h build) / PCBWay (PETG, instant quote) / Craftcloud (no minimum). Metrix Create:Space closed 2018 (per article headline/URL; page fetch blocked); SoDo MakerSpace domain is dead; SPL public 3D printing: not checked (unverified).

## Big-box notes (Home Depot / Lowe's) - pages could not be fetched (403); treat as estimates

- PETG adhesive: Loctite Plastics Bonding System (primer + CA) or Gorilla Super Glue Gel; both are standard shelf items. Better: Amazon for a 3D-print-specific CA such as Starbond/Insta-Cure; **estimate**.
- Aluminum foil tape (Nashua 322 / 3M 425 style): standard HVAC aisle, 2 in x 10 yd; fine locally.
- Silicone tubing: big-box "food-grade" 1/4 in OD is usually clear PVC/vinyl (Everbilt), not silicone, and 1/8 in ID x 1/4 in OD is not a stocked size - **buy online**. Vinyl 3/16 in ID is OK for inlet/outlet outside the pump head.
- Hook-and-loop straps (Velcro One-Wrap), cable ties, M3 Hillman drawer packs, Master Magnetics neodymium discs: all standard big-box items; local is fine.
- Panel toggle/rocker switches: big-box automotive aisle has 12 V rockers; Vetco selection is better and cheaper.
- Better online only: heat-set inserts, XIAO RP2040, DRV5032 breakout, 1/8 x 1/4 silicone, exact-spec springs (McMaster), D2F-L microswitches.
