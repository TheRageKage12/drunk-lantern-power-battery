    Leg-bending drawing and soldering access

    The download is a full-size drawing of the part and its metal legs, from three angles. Print at 100% and use it to check the bends before cutting.

    How to find a hole on the board

    H04 means column H, row 4. Letters run across the drawing; numbers run down it. These are this guide’s labels, not necessarily markings on your board. On the component side, A01 is the upper-left grid hole. Flip left-to-right to solder: A01 moves to the upper right, but its name stays the same. Match the mounting holes to the drawing and keep row 01 at the top.

    Wire soldering technique

    Strip just enough to reach the joint shown on the back. Gently twist the strands and insert them without pre-tinning; keep the insulation against the front face. Solder the wire to the copper ring around its entry hole. Bend the bare end against the destination pin and trim it just beyond the pin. Reheat the pin’s existing solder to attach the wire. Keep stray strands away from neighbouring joints.

    Later enclosure wiring · fixed cut list

    Separate pieces for connecting the board to the rest of the lantern. Cut each listed colour; do not add these lengths to the board leads above. Retain connector pigtails and trim the new wire to meet them.
