      How to find a hole on the board

      H04 means column H, row 4. Letters run across the drawing; numbers run down it. These are this guide’s labels, not necessarily markings on your board. On the component side, A01 is the upper-left grid hole. Flip left-to-right to solder: A01 moves to the upper right, but its name stays the same. Match the mounting holes to the drawing and keep row 01 at the top.

      Wire soldering technique

      Strip just enough to reach the joint shown on the back. Gently twist the strands and insert them without pre-tinning; keep the insulation against the front face. Solder the wire’s hole, bend its short bare end to the destination joint, then solder there and clip any excess. Keep stray strands away from neighbouring joints.
