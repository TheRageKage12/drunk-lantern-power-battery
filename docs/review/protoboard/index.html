How to read this diagram

Dashed squares and circles: mounting screw clearances and holes to drill. Keep parts and wires outside each square.

Pad: the copper soldering spot around a board hole. A09 means column A, row 09. Net: points joined electrically by wires, such as all ground connections.

Click a coloured soldering spot to read its connection. Use Show W… in the wire list to highlight one wire and its two soldering holes. Use + to enlarge both drawings and Fit to reset.

Solder side — wiring

Rear wiring: crossings stay insulated. Solder only at the marked endpoints. W01/W02/W05–W07 are bare copper links between adjacent holes. Dashed coloured lines are cables leaving the board. Pins marked NC have no wire attached. Small solid squares mark cable attachments or wire junctions.

Build limits: verify the actual board's hole positions and C2 dimensions first. Keep rear wires below the 10 mm gap and outside the screw areas. Disconnect all power while soldering. Remove JP1 for USB. See the assembly instructions for checks.
