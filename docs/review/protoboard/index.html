How to read this diagram

Dashed outlines and circles: mounting screw clearances and holes to drill. Keep parts and wires outside each marked clearance.

Pad: the copper soldering spot around a board hole. A09 means column A, row 09. Net: points joined electrically by wires, such as all ground connections.

Click a coloured soldering spot to read its connection. Use Show W… in the wire list to highlight one wire and its solder points. Use + to enlarge both drawings and Fit to reset.
