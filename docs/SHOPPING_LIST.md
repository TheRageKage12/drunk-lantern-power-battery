# Shopping list by store

The same parts as section 2 of the [build guide](BUILD_GUIDE.md), grouped by where you buy them. Prices with
"~" are estimates; confirm at checkout. Tick the boxes as you go.

## Amazon (one order, ~$124)

- [ ] **Pump**: [Kamoer KPHM200, 12 V (B09HGVLHFV)](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09HGVLHFV), $28
- [ ] **Tubing**: [AUBER 1/8" ID x 1/4" OD platinum silicone, 10 ft (B0DKB1NKB4)](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DKB1NKB4), $14. Exactly this size; thinner tube will not pump.
- [ ] **Battery**: [KBT 12 V 1200 mAh Li-ion pack with charger (B0C241NS29)](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C241NS29), $22
- [ ] **Microcontroller**: [Seeed XIAO RP2040, pre-soldered (B0DRNTQ338)](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DRNTQ338), ~$10
- [ ] **LED strip**: [12 V green, single colour](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=12V+green+LED+strip+single+color+2835+60+LED), ~$12. 12 V, green only (not RGB), 2835 LEDs, 60/m, 8 mm wide, cuttable every 5 cm.
- [ ] **Buck converter**: [LM2596 module, multipack](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=LM2596+buck+converter+module), ~$8
- [ ] **Magnets**: [6 x 2 mm neodymium, 45-pack (B0CBRVF4PN)](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CBRVF4PN), ~$7
- [ ] **Cable plugs**: [JST-SM 2-pin and 4-pin connector pairs](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=JST+SM+2+pin+4+pin+connector+kit), ~$7
- [ ] **Vent valve**: [Aquaneat aquarium check valves, 10-pack](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Aquaneat+aquarium+check+valve+one+way), ~$6. Or the aquarium aisle of a pet store.
- [ ] **Curtain**: [COYOUCO 0.5 mm frosted PP sheets (B0FM3YRGG4)](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0FM3YRGG4), ~$10. Or one frosted poly report cover from an office store.

## Adafruit (optional, ~$13 with shipping)

- [ ] **Hall sensor**: [DRV5032 breakout #6051](https://www.adafruit.com/product/6051), $4.95 + ~$8 shipping

Or skip this order: buy an **A3144** at Vetco ($1.99) instead (see guide section 2.1; it needs a diode and two
stacked magnets).

- [ ] **DC5521 panel-mount jack** (5.5 x 2.1 mm female, M8 nut), ~$4. Plus a **DC5521 male plug with bare leads** if the battery didn't come with one.
- [ ] **Compression spring**: 0.5 mm wire, 5 mm OD, ~10 mm long (Hillman), $1.29
- [ ] **Small parts**, ~$12:

  - [ ] Resistors: 2 x 10 k, 1 x 100 k, 1 x 22 k
  - [ ] 2 x 0.1 µF ceramic capacitors
  - [ ] 1 x 2200 µF 16 V electrolytic capacitor

  - [ ] Heat-shrink

## Only if you don't have them

- [ ] **PETG**: ~0.85 kg green plus a little white or natural (~$40 for two spools)
- [ ] **Jar**: food-safe, max 76 mm across and 76 mm tall, e.g. an 8 oz straight-sided PET jar (~$8; Micro Homebrew, Kenmore)
- [ ] **Shot glass**: 1.5 oz, max 52 mm across the top and 62 mm tall (Libbey 1.5 oz fits; Bargreen Ellingson, SoDo)
- [ ] **Tools**: soldering iron, multimeter, calipers, drill with 2.5 mm and 6 mm bits, flush cutters

**Total: about $180** (Amazon ~$124, Adafruit ~$13, Vetco ~$30, Tacoma Screw ~$12, glue ~$5).
