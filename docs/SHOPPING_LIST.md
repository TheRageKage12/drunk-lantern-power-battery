# Shopping list by store

The same parts as section 2 of the [build guide](BUILD_GUIDE.md), grouped by where you buy them. Vetco prices
are from its website (October 2026); prices with "~" are estimates, so confirm at checkout. Links marked
*search* open a search; pick a listing that matches the spec given. Tick the boxes as you go.

## Amazon (one order, ~$132)

- [ ] **Pump**: [Kamoer KPHM200, 12 V (B09HGVLHFV)](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09HGVLHFV), $28
- [ ] **Tubing**: [AUBER 1/8" ID x 1/4" OD platinum silicone, 10 ft (B0DKB1NKB4)](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DKB1NKB4), $14. Exactly this size; thinner tube will not pump.
- [ ] **Battery**: [KBT 12 V 1200 mAh Li-ion pack with charger (B0C241NS29)](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C241NS29), $22
- [ ] **Microcontroller**: [Seeed XIAO RP2040, pre-soldered (B0DRNTQ338)](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DRNTQ338), ~$10
- [ ] **Lever microswitch, D2F-L size, 4-pack**: [B0DBV8XGG4](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DBV8XGG4), ~$8. Body 12.8 x 5.8 x 6.5 mm, holes 6.5 mm apart, hinge lever. (Vetco has lever microswitches but doesn't list their sizes; this one is known to fit the lock.)
- [ ] **LED strip**: [12 V green, single colour (*search*)](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=12V+green+LED+strip+single+color+2835+60+LED), ~$12. 12 V, green only (not RGB), 2835 LEDs, 60/m, 8 mm wide, cuttable every 5 cm.
- [ ] **Buck converter**: [LM2596 module, multipack (*search*)](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=LM2596+buck+converter+module), ~$8
- [ ] **Magnets**: [6 x 2 mm neodymium, 45-pack (B0CBRVF4PN)](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CBRVF4PN), ~$7
- [ ] **Cable plugs**: [JST-SM 2-pin and 4-pin connector pairs (*search*)](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=JST+SM+2+pin+4+pin+connector+kit), ~$7
- [ ] **Vent valve**: [Aquaneat aquarium check valves, 10-pack (*search*)](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Aquaneat+aquarium+check+valve+one+way), ~$6. Or the aquarium aisle of a pet store.
- [ ] **Curtain**: [COYOUCO 0.5 mm frosted PP sheets (B0FM3YRGG4)](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0FM3YRGG4), ~$10. Or one frosted poly report cover from an office store.

[12718 Northup Way STE 100](https://vetco.net), (425) 641-7275. Mon-Fri 9-6, Sat 10-3:30.

- [ ] **Gate driver**: [74AHCT125 quad level-shifter (VUPN524)](https://vetco.net/products/vupn524_quad_level-shifter_-_74ahct125_-_3v_to_5v_d96), $2.95. It must be **AHCT**, not HC.
- [ ] **2 x MOSFET**: [IRLZ44N logic-level (VUPN1682)](https://vetco.net/products/vupn1682_mosfet_mosft_for_micro_controllers_-_30v_-_60a_-_irlb8721pbf_d151), $2.67 each. The page title says IRLZ44N and its web address says IRLB8721; both are logic-level and have the same pin order, so either works.
- [ ] **Power switch**: [SPST mini toggle, on-off, with wire leads](https://vetco.net/products/30-10005_spst_miniature_toggle_switch_w-wire_leads_on-off_6a), $4.95. 1/4" bushing, 6 A at 12 V.

- [ ] **Spring**: [Hillman compression spring (*search*)](https://vetco.net/search?q=hillman+compression+spring), ~$1.29. Pick by calipers: 0.5 mm wire, 5 mm outside diameter, about 10 mm long.
